## Part 1 — Performance Pass

### Lazy split rationale

| Route | File | Size | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| /courses/:id/learn | learning-page.tsx | 35 kB | Lazy — auth-only, never needed on first paint |
| /habits | habits-page.tsx | 23 kB | Lazy — auth-only, anonymous visitors never reach it |
| /courses/:id | course-detail-page.tsx | 21 kB | Lazy — only reached after courses list |
| / sign-in sign-up | small pages | <4 kB | Keep eager — first-load entry points |

### Files to modify
- src/App.tsx — React.lazy() + Suspense wrapping all lazy routes
- src/components/common/page-skeleton.tsx — full-page pulse skeleton (CREATE)
- src/sections/coursesection.tsx — add loading="lazy" + width/height to below-fold images
